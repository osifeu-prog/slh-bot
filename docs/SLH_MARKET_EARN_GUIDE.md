# SLH Market — User Earn & Use Guide

## Product goal

Make SLH understandable to a normal user without requiring Telegram commands or crypto expertise.

The user should always be able to answer four questions:

1. What do I have?
2. How can I earn or receive more?
3. What can I use it for?
4. What should I do next?

## Primary navigation

- Home — current balances and the next recommended system step.
- Wallet — Credits, SLH, staking status and wallet state.
- Earn — available, verified reward opportunities.
- Buy — approved purchase flows.
- Stake — staking explanation, terms and confirmation.
- Market — verified utility/trading surfaces only.
- Alpha — readiness evidence and policy status.
- Academy — learning and task progress.
- SLH AI — contextual help based on the authenticated user's actual state.

## Earn UX

Every earning opportunity must show:

- Action required
- Reward type
- Reward amount, only when an authoritative policy exists
- Eligibility conditions
- Completion state
- Settlement status
- Any limits or expiry

Do not display a reward amount based only on legacy code, historical screenshots, or old tokenomics documents.

## Receive UX

The Receive screen must distinguish:

- Internal Credits
- Internal SLH balance
- On-chain SLH
- AIR/Alpha allocation

A user must never be led to believe that an internal balance is an on-chain asset or that an Alpha preview is a guaranteed allocation.

## Use / market UX

Possible surfaces are shown only when their backend authority is verified:

- Pay for approved services
- Marketplace purchases
- Verified transfers
- Staking
- Other approved ecosystem utilities

A button must not be shown as available merely because a historical API or legacy module exists.

## AI guidance

The AI assistant should answer in contextual, user-specific terms:

- current balance
- current progress
- available verified actions
- what is missing
- explanation of risks/conditions

The AI must not invent allocation amounts, guarantee profits, bypass permissions, or turn historical policy into current policy.

## Alpha / AIR

Current Alpha UI is evidence/readiness only until an authoritative policy defines:

- eligibility
- allocation formula
- allocation budget
- distribution schedule
- current token allocation constraints

Until then, allocation must remain visibly `policy_missing` and no amount should be promised.

## Product principle

The long-term product vision is broad SLH adoption and utility. The UX should make receiving, earning, using and understanding SLH progressively easier while keeping financial claims, token issuance and distribution governed by verified policy and authorization.
