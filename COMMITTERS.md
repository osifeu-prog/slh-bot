# SLH OS Committers

Public engineering credit is tied to merged pull requests in the canonical repository.

## How a contributor is listed

A developer is added after their first merged Developer Lab PR. The entry records the public GitHub identity and the merged contribution; no private Telegram data is published.

## Current contributors

_The public contributor list will be populated from the first verified merged Developer Lab PR._

## Reward policy

- First merged PR: 5,000 internal Credits
- Each additional merged PR: 2,500 internal Credits
- After 3 merged PRs: OWNER may discuss an optional SLH bonus from existing supply

Credits are ledger-issued internal units. SLH bonuses, when offered, are transfers from existing supply and are not minting or a contractual promise.
